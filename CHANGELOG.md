# Changelog

## v04-bottom-access — 2026-09-11

Source and website update on top of v0.4.0-alpha; no new release tag.
**Physically unvalidated prototype.**

- Removed the two narrow bottom bridges, joining USB-C and side service windows.
- Matched the approved study geometry exactly; lid and all other meshes unchanged.
- Added current Blender, STL, 3MF and viewer files, reproducible patch/export
  script and digital validation report. Existing baseline files remain historical.
- Body remains manifold; all 18 sampled access rays pass. STL round trips confirm
  millimetre dimensions, and the 3MF contains two objects with explicit mm units.
- Replaced old downloads on Printables and Thingiverse, activated the new
  Sketchfab viewer, and retained existing uploaded gallery renders.
- Actual-device alignment, acoustics, cable clearance and strength remain untested.

## v0.4.0-alpha — 2026-09-10

Design v04 adds fuller coverage and explicit compliant-pad retention provisions.
**Physically unvalidated prototype, not a certified frictionless opening.**

- Deeper rear lid/body wrapping with shaped hinge-motion relief.
- Bounded 10 × 7 mm front window, separate 14 × 8 mm USB-C and two 6 × 4 mm
  speaker windows instead of the broad v03 cutouts.
- Five recessed seats for separately supplied compliant pads: two body-side,
  two lid-side and one roof seat. Bare rigid shells remain unretained.
- A 0.80 mm soft foam pad is a starting trial, not a validated fit specification.
- Preserved silhouette, pixel branding, 4 mm keyring bore and outer dimensions.
- 116 discrete hinge poses, 0–115° at 1° steps, pass the surrogate collision
  screen with seated-pad previews. No physical opening/friction test performed.
- Updated editable source, print exports, renders and digital reports. v03 files
  and the v0.3.0-alpha release remain available as historical artifacts.

## v0.3.0-alpha — 2026-09-09

First public source release, based on design revision v03. **Physically
unvalidated prototype.** The two manufacturing meshes match the private v03
prototype; preparing the public package is not a new fit or mechanical revision.

- Rounded Ø4 mm diagonal right-side keyring bore.
- Robust nominal 1 mm-deep pixel rear lettering, inherited from v02.
- Editable Blender source, JSON parameters, Python build/validation tools and
  explicitly dimensioned STL/3MF exports.
- Credited CC BY input assets and MIT / CC BY-SA project licensing.
- Non-open donor packages and hidden donor meshes excluded; clean reconstruction
  imports only the credited Clawd and AirPods sources.
- Separate printing guidance and physical-qualification checklist.

### Earlier construction stages

- v02: robust rear pixel lettering, without changing the fit cavity.
- v01: initial two-piece Clawd cover and provisional visual-model fit envelope.
