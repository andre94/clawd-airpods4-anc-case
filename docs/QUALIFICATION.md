# Physical qualification — pending

No real-device or physical-print test has been completed for this release.
The downloaded AirPods visual model is not measured OEM CAD. Published Apple
overall case dimensions (50.1 × 21.2 × 46.2 mm) do not specify local contours,
hinge location, opening forces or manufacturing tolerances.

## Current evidence

- The two release meshes have closed, consistently oriented geometry and one
  connected component each in the recorded digital checks.
- Discrete surrogate-based insertion and hinge sweeps pass the recorded checks.
- v04 passes 116 hinge poses from 0–115° at 1° intervals, including illustrative
  seated pads. This is a collision screen, not proof of frictionless opening,
  real hinge alignment, foam deformation or a continuously swept clearance.
- The keyring envelope check is a geometric screen, not a threading simulation,
  minimum-wall certification, fatigue test or load rating.
- No supplier's approval, physical fit, protective performance, drop resistance,
  wireless charging behavior or hardware retention is established by those checks.

## Record for each prototype

Record exact release/commit and file SHA-256, printer/provider, process, material,
orientation, dimensional settings, finish and any post-processing. Use measured
values when available; mark visual estimates explicitly. Never put addresses,
phone numbers, invoices, order numbers or private correspondence in a public issue.

For v04, also record pad material, uncompressed thickness, adhesive backing,
placement, removability and changes after compression/wear. The five pad seats
and suggested 0.80 mm starting trial do not constitute a tested retention system.

- [ ] Inspect the empty print for sharp edges, loose powder/supports and cracks.
- [ ] Confirm overall dimensions and local clearances; check cavity condition.
- [ ] Trial gentle insertion/removal without force or leverage on connectors.
- [ ] Verify body retention and **separate lid retention** independently.
- [ ] Check pad surface compatibility, compression, adhesive residue and safe
      removal; test a removable roof bond separately if one is needed.
- [ ] Check full lid opening, hold-open behavior and earbud removal by hand.
- [ ] Check the actual USB-C plug without loading the charging port.
- [ ] Verify status LED visibility and pairing/reset front-tap operation.
- [ ] Verify ANC Find My speaker audibility through the cover.
- [ ] Check Qi and Apple Watch charging alignment, attachment and temperature;
      stop on unusual heating, interrupted charging or other abnormal behavior.
- [ ] Check the actual split ring's wire dimensions, threading and articulation
      without scratching or stressing the plastic.
- [ ] Evaluate pull, twist, snag and wear with an **empty cover or dummy first**.
      Do not use valuable electronics for unqualified load or drop tests.
- [ ] Recheck retention after wear, finishing and any removable liner adjustment.

The keyring attaches only to the outer body. It does not lock the AirPods case
inside it or secure the separate cover lid. There is **no rated carrying load**.
Passing one sample does not establish reliability for different materials or
printers. Document failures and revisions rather than silently scaling files.
