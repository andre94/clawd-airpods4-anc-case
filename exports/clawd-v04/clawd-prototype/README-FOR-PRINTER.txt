CLAWD AIRPODS 4 ANC — v04 FULLER COVERAGE / PAD-RETENTION PROTOTYPE
10 September 2026. UNVALIDATED PHYSICAL PROTOTYPE; NOT A PRODUCTION RELEASE.

PRINT ONE BODY AND ONE LID, using either:
  clawd-prototype-body-v04-mm.stl
  clawd-prototype-lid-v04-mm.stl
OR:
  clawd-prototype-pair-v04-mm.3mf (contains both parts; not an extra pair)

UNITS: millimetres. Import STLs at 100%, one coordinate unit per mm.
3MF explicitly specifies millimetres. No automatic scaling, thickening or repair.
Expected bounding boxes:
  Body: 75.778 x 26.900 x 42.396 mm
  Lid:  62.000 x 26.900 x 16.504 mm
These are separate part bounds, not the assembled height.

CANDIDATE MATERIAL: unfilled MJF PA12, cleaned and unpainted for first fit testing.
Terracotta colour, black eyes, charcoal pads and metal ring in renders are
illustrative. Do not print a metal ring or the charcoal pad-preview geometry.
Keep dimensional fit surfaces uncoated for the first prototype.

REQUIRED SEPARATE HARDWARE, NOT IN THE PRINT FILES:
  Two compliant body-side pads, projected footprint 8 x 8 mm each.
  Two compliant lid-side pads, projected footprint 8 x 5 mm each.
  One compliant lid-roof pad, projected footprint 16 x 7 mm.
The seats follow curved surfaces; footprints are not flattened cutting patterns.
Nominal pad-seat clearance is 0.65 mm normal to the visual surrogate; the rest
of the cavity retains the provisional 0.35 mm clearance. Start by evaluating
0.80 mm soft, non-marking silicone foam or equivalent, including its backing.
Final pad material and thickness MUST follow printed-gap and retention tests.
Pads bond to the cover; a removable, finish-compatible double-sided roof pad
may be evaluated if friction alone is inadequate. No permanent glue.

THE BARE RIGID SHELLS ARE NOT SELF-RETAINING. No positive latch or retention
force is specified. Do not force the device in or use real AirPods for pull tests.

FEATURES TO REVIEW:
  Fuller rear skirts and shaped 0–115 degree surrogate hinge clearances.
  Bounded 10 x 7 mm front LED/pairing window.
  14 x 8 mm USB-C opening and two 6 x 4 mm speaker-access windows.
  Five recessed pad seats; approximately 2.10 mm minimum sampled roof backing.
  Existing 4 mm diagonal keyring bore with rounded entries.
  Existing 1 mm-deep pixel lettering.
The sampled backing value is NOT a global wall-thickness certification.
Service locations and cable access are provisional, not physically measured.
Completely depowder the cavities, recesses and bore. Flag inadequate walls or
unreachable cleaning areas before manufacture; do not silently modify them.

DIGITAL QA: watertight single-component meshes, positive volume, topology and
triangle-overlap screens, rigid-shell axial insertion, 116 sampled hinge poses
including seated pad previews, STL round trips and millimetre 3MF checks pass.
This is NOT proof of real fit, compliant-pad behaviour, retention, hinge travel,
acoustic access, charging performance, impact resistance or anchor strength.

Physically qualify device/cap retention, easy removal, full hinge movement,
earbud access, LED/pairing taps, USB-C, ANC speaker and wireless charging.
Do not suspend real AirPods from the ring until retention and anchor tests pass.
