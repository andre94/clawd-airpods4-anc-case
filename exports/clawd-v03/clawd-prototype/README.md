# v03 prototype print files — physically unvalidated

Print one body and one lid. Use **either both STLs or the two-part 3MF**; do not
order duplicate parts by uploading all three files. Units are millimetres at
100% scale. The 3MF is mesh-only, not a qualified slicer profile.

- Body: 75.78 × 26.90 × 42.40 mm.
- Lid: 62.00 × 26.90 × 16.50 mm.
- No device, ring, paint or assembly hardware is included.

Read [printing guidance](../../../docs/PRINTING.md) and the
[physical qualification checklist](../../../docs/QUALIFICATION.md) before
ordering. Ask your printer to assess cavity tolerances, thin walls, engraving
and keyring strength. First print unpainted; fit and retention are untested.
An untested cover must not be relied on to carry the device.
