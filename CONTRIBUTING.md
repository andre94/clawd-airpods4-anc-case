# Contributing

This project welcomes reproducible improvements and honest prototype reports.
Please start an issue for fit, manufacturing or retention findings, identifying
the exact revision, process, material and finishing. State what you actually
tested and distinguish measurements from estimates.

For CAD changes, include the editable source, parameters, derived meshes,
updated attribution and a concise description of the changed geometry. Record
units, dimensions and digital validation results. Keep the two print parts
separate from reference and preview objects. Do not describe a revision as
print-ready or safe to carry until its physical evidence supports that statement.

For scripts, keep dependencies minimal and document how to run them. Preserve
the guard against overwriting someone else's model or resetting an interactive
Blender session. A compile check is not a CAD rebuild or a physical test.

By contributing, you agree to license your contributions under the applicable
project license: MIT for code/workflows, CC BY-SA 4.0 for design/documentation.
Only contribute work you have the right to share. Identify any upstream author,
source, license and modifications; do not replace upstream notices with ours.

Never post credentials, private CAD, personal shipping details, private messages
or copyrighted models without redistribution permission. If you find a rights
or privacy concern, contact the maintainer through the public contact options at
[andreabalbo.com](https://andreabalbo.com) without reposting the sensitive data.
